MCQ Challenge: Tenses

Test your knowledge with these competition-style questions!

1. Which sentence is in the present perfect continuous tense?
  • I finished my homework.
  • I have been working on this project for two hours.
  • I will be going to the market.
  • I had completed the task before they arrived.
Correct! "I have been working" is present perfect continuous tense.
Incorrect. The correct answer is "I have been working on this project for two hours."
2. Choose the correct future perfect tense form:
  • She will go to school tomorrow.
  • She will be going to school tomorrow.
  • She will have gone to school by 10 AM tomorrow.
  • She goes to school every day.
